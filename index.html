A manicure and pedicure together

Our menu includes a simultaneous manicure and pedicure option for an additional $10. Please confirm availability when booking.

Keeping your set fresh

The listed refill window for nail and lash extensions is within three weeks. If you’re coming from another salon, contact us first: your existing set may require removal and a new set.

Planning for a special occasion

Makeup and hair services are available for everyday moments, special evenings and weddings. House calls carry an additional fee—contact the salon for a quote.
